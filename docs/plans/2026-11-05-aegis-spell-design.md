# AEGIS Spell Design

AEGIS adds a defensive decision without adding another input key. Its rhythm is one step, one whole-beat rest, then finish: `STEP · REST · FINISH`. This contrasts with FLARE's immediate finish and MEND's repeated step while staying readable on the same four-position public beat ring.

The command parser recognizes AEGIS when one accepted step is followed by a finish exactly two whole beats later. A completed AEGIS arms the battle model's existing one-hit shield. The shield persists until the next boss strike, then absorbs all damage and is consumed. Timing grade does not change whether the shield works; a merely good cast remains strategically useful.

The tutorial expands from six to nine bars. Bars 0–2 teach FLARE, bars 3–5 teach MEND, and bars 6–8 teach AEGIS. Bar 8 is a scripted teaching strike, allowing the player to see the shield block damage immediately. Regular phase-based attacks begin after that demonstration.

All three spell cards share the bottom row. Each compact card shows its spell name, effect, and rhythm diagram; the global footer continues to explain the two controls. The AEGIS card and shield effect use cyan so the defensive state reads consistently across the recipe, player barrier, and blocked-hit feedback.
