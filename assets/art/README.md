# Art assets

The two prototype art assets in this folder were generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool on 2026-11-05. They are original AI-generated prototype material and contain no third-party logos or readable text.

The active Beatspell arena and characters use three 16-bit pixel assets generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-11-09. See [pixel assets and complete prompts](beatspell/pixel/README.md). The painted v0.1.1 assets are superseded historical material. HUD ornaments, sigils and 3×5/5×7 bitmap lettering are procedural GDScript. No external font binaries are redistributed.

## Underground passage

File: `subway/subway_background.png`

Final prompt: Create a production-ready 16:9 side-view pixel-art background for a 2D light rhythm game: an underground pedestrian passage at night with tiled walls, concrete reflective floor, vending machine, utility doors, abstract unreadable posters and overhead fluorescent lights. Keep the center and lower center clear for gameplay. Use authentic modern 16-bit pixel art with hard square pixels, deliberate clusters, a cold blue-gray and muted purple palette, sparse cyan and magenta neon accents, and no UI, readable text, logos or watermark.

## Loop

File: `loop/loop_idle.png`

Final prompt: Create one isolated full-body transparent pixel-art character named Loop for a 2D light rhythm game. Loop is an androgynous young solo street musician with short dark-blue hair, oversized charcoal hoodie, tapered trousers, high-top shoes, headphones and a cross-body equipment pouch. Use a relaxed three-quarter pose, visible hands, a strong neutral silhouette, authentic modern 16-bit pixel clusters, cold blue-gray and purple clothing with cyan and magenta accents, genuine transparent alpha, and no scenery, props, text, logo or watermark.
